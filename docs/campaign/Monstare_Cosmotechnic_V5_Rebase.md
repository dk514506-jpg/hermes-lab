# Motivational Ecology — Monstare Cosmotechnic v5 Rebase

**Date:** 2026-08-14  
**Status:** current architectural position; replaces the campaign's earlier assumption that the Motivational Ecology estate is itself a finished human-facing operating instrument.

## Why this rebase exists

The Monstare evidence matrix is now complete at 129/129 charted rows, with the Epithet Register and fault-line memos carrying the project's current source texture. A hostile Council/Locus review of `The_Monstare_v4_Built_Artifacts.docx` found that v4 is conceptually serious but operationally inadmissible: it has contradictory minimums, two competing lineages, an underspecified safety route, hidden/optional measurement logic, unsupported thresholds, and an apparatus burden that its own ethic does not yet survive.

The correct response is not to add more cards to v4. It is to preserve v4 as an exploratory archive and rebuild the runtime surface around a smaller Monstare v5 Kernel.

## Current Monstare position

Monstare is a **Tier-P cosmotechnic instrument**, not a productivity optimizer. Its telos is productivity-supporting cosmotechnic cultivation: helping a person examine and revise their relation to activity, attention, body, environment, and meaning without converting the person into a metric target.

The current operative commitments are:

- **S1 Telic:** no hidden shift to throughput, engagement, compliance, or output maximization.
- **S7 Moral:** no manipulation, shame, extraction, concealed power, or effectiveness-based justification of harm.
- **S10 Reversibility:** pause, refuse, revise, switch, seek support, export, and exit remain legitimate.
- **S4 Phenomenological:** describe before interpreting; lived experience is not a variable merely because the system can label it.
- **S9 Burden:** burden is an outcome to test, not a timing promise or a user's character defect.
- **S11 Offloading:** assistance must preserve planning, evaluation, context, and refusal.
- **S12 Staleness:** fast-moving AI/offloading evidence remains a living evidence obligation.

## The v5 Kernel

`Monstare_v5_Kernel.md` is the current user-facing architectural prototype. It uses one canonical lineage: time-led, user-authored, reversible. The former state-led Chosen Object lineage is archived and is not runtime-compatible with v5.

The kernel has four decisive gates:

1. **Safety first:** yes or unsure stops the Monstare route and seeks appropriate external/professional support. Safety bypasses self-management routing.
2. **Description before interpretation:** free description precedes any optional classification; unknown, mixed, or cannot-tell are valid.
3. **Explicit state vocabulary:** return, pause, switch, care, support, parked, unresolved, and switched-method.
4. **No hidden thresholds:** no automatic three-event or timer escalation, no streaks, no performance score, and no claim that a research measurement firewall exists before its protocol is built.

The kernel is not validated and does not diagnose, triage clinically, or establish causal effectiveness.

## What the campaign now means by cosmotechnics

Cosmotechnics is not decorative language added to a coaching pipeline. It is a governance question about what cosmos, moral order, technical relation, and form of life an artifact presupposes and enacts.

The Cosmotechnic Audit must therefore ask:

- What is the artifact's effective telos?
- What locality, moral order, and human-technical relation does it presuppose?
- What does it universalize, and what does it leave culturally unresolved?
- What politics, identity, and power relation does it mediate or inscribe?
- What does the artifact enable and erode as pharmakon?
- Where is its margin of indeterminacy and user revisability?
- Can the person pause, refuse, revise, export, and exit without penalty?

These questions are criterion-bearing and source-grounded, not a scalar cosmotechnic score. Hui/technodiversity and universal autonomy, Stiegler/pharmakon and digital support, Simondon/Winner, Stiegler/Heidegger, Hui/Haraway, and Feenberg/Ellul remain disagreements or open fault lines; the harness must not smooth them into a single doctrine.

## Revised relationship between FAOS and Ecology

The earlier campaign language described FAOS as gateway infrastructure and Ecology as a “Guardian intellect.” That remains useful as a systems metaphor, but it must not authorize an agent to become a guardian over the person.

- **FAOS** may govern epistemic/process cleanliness: provenance, evidence ladder, route validity, state lineage, quarantine, and instrumented close.
- **Ecology/Monstare** governs the conditions under which an action may be proposed toward a person: authorship, refusal, burden, reversibility, safety, and non-extraction.
- **The person remains the authority over their own telos and meaning.** Ecology may not supply identity, meaning, diagnosis, or a hidden intervention.
- FAOS clearing a claim never licenses its use toward a person. A trustworthy claim may still be impermissible to use in that relationship.
- A declared safeguard is not an enforced safeguard. Every campaign document must distinguish machine-enforced, deterministic verifier-checked, user-practice, and untested design-hypothesis status.

The next runtime seam is not a larger coaching stack. It is a tested interface between a minimal kernel and any future FAOS/agentic machinery, with explicit stop, support, and unresolved routes.

## Retained campaign strengths

The Valens-derived discipline remains a method discipline, not evidence for cosmotechnic findings: preserve conflicting witnesses, label source versus inference, quarantine weak claims, treat worked examples as test vectors, and record dissent.

The earlier Ecology strengths remain valuable: user-owned meaning, no automatic reward-technique selection, identity-level confirmation, arrangement rather than imposed meaning, CMO hypotheses rather than verdicts, two-typed quarantine, and instrumented close.

## Superseded assumptions

The following older campaign statements must not be repeated without qualification:

- that the coaching estate is a finished human-facing operating instrument;
- that a deterministic driver demonstration is a genuine human-facing live session;
- that a prose gate or measurement firewall is runtime enforcement;
- that an elaborate multi-card architecture is low-burden merely because modules are called optional;
- that “Guardian intellect” may decide how the human should proceed;
- that COM-B, BCW/BCT, SDT, or practice-theory mappings automatically license Monstare interventions;
- that a Q-tier or evidence clearance authorizes use toward the person;
- that v4's dual lineage can be left as a user choice without state migration controls.

## Current next gate

Before expanding the agentic harness or adding second-wave Monstare cards:

1. exercise the v5 Kernel on one concrete component with a fresh reader;
2. include a safety-uncertainty case and an ambiguous/multiple-state case;
3. record burden, invented local policy, refusal/support behavior, and carry-forward state;
4. distinguish ordinary use from any separately consented research measurement;
5. run Locus/Council review and record dissent before promotion.

No causal or general effectiveness claim may be drawn from that exercise.
