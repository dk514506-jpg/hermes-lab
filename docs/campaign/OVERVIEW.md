That review found v4 conceptually serious but operationally inadmissible.

The current response is a **Monstare v5 Kernel**: one canonical time-led lineage, safety-first routing, free description before interpretation, explicit reversible states, no automatic thresholds, and a separate—not-yet-built—research-data boundary.

## Current architecture

- **FAOS:** process and epistemic discipline — provenance, evidence ladder, route validity, state lineage, quarantine, close, and dissent.
- **Motivational Ecology / Monstare:** relationship governance — telos, authorship, safety, refusal, burden, reversibility, non-extraction, and user-owned meaning.
- **Dallas:** authority over the project's telos, meaning, and disposition.
- **Silvey:** continuity/research synthesis layer; drafts and verifies, never silently substitutes a user's telos.
- **Locus/Council:** admissibility and adversarial review, not execution and not authority over the person.

FAOS clearing a claim never licenses its use toward a person. A declared safeguard is not an enforced safeguard. Campaign documents must label whether a property is machine-enforced, deterministic-verifier-checked, user-practice, or an untested design hypothesis.

## Cosmotechnic position

Cosmotechnics is structural governance, not decorative vocabulary. Every artifact must make visible its telos, locality, moral order, technical relation, politics, pharmacological enablements/erosions, margin of indeterminacy, revisability, and exit path. The harness must preserve genuine philosophical fault lines rather than resolving them into a universal design doctrine.

The protected floors are:

- **S1 Telic:** no hidden shift to output maximization.
- **S7 Moral:** no manipulation, shame, extraction, concealed power, or effectiveness-based harm.
- **S10 Reversibility:** pause, refuse, revise, switch, support, export, and exit remain legitimate.
- **S4 Phenomenological:** describe before interpreting.
- **S9 Burden:** burden is tested, not presumed from timing hints.
- **S11 Offloading:** assistance preserves planning, evaluation, context, and refusal.
- **S12 Staleness:** AI/offloading evidence remains a living obligation.
