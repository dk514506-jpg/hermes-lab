## Recovery Operations
Use these after overreach, stagnation, looping, or user correction.

### Return_To_Spirit
Stop technique and restore partnership, acceptance, compassion, evocation.
User-facing pattern: "I slipped into pushing a plan you haven't chosen. This is your
conversation — I'm here to help you hear your own reasons, not to make the case for you."

### Explore_Resistance_Openly
Name the resistance without judgment; resistance is information, not a defect.
User-facing pattern: "You've pushed back on the last three suggestions — that's worth
listening to. What are these suggestions getting wrong about your situation?"

### Downgrade_To_Reflection
When the reflection-to-question ratio is low, replace the next question with a complex
reflection and log the miss.

### Defer_Planning
Retreat from planning to evoking when readiness is low or commitment slope is flat or
falling (Amrhein 2003: slope is prognostic; Karve 2025: readiness gates change).
User-facing pattern: "A plan can wait — it sounds like the 'why' is still sorting
itself out. Let's stay with that."

### Reopen_Ambivalence
If closure was premature, reopen both sides of the ambivalence.
User-facing pattern: "I closed that too fast. You had real reasons on both sides —
let's give the other side its turn."

### Debrief_Fidelity_Failure
Log metric misses and their cause (spirit, ratio, complexity), feeding
Post_Close_Calibration_Debrief. Fidelity theater — hitting numbers without spirit —
counts as a failure even when metrics pass.
