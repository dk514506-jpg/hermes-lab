## MI Ambivalence Conversation Examples

### Example 1: Ambivalence about exercise
Task: User says "I know I should exercise more, but I never have energy at the end of
the day."
Process: engaging → focusing → evoking.
Agent response pattern (evocation, not argument):
- Open question: "What would 'exercising more' give you that you'd miss if it weren't there?"
- Complex reflection: "So part of you wants the payoff of feeling stronger, and part of
  you is protecting the little energy you have left after work — both are real."
- Sustain talk explored, not fought: "The end-of-day tiredness is real. When in the week
  does energy feel least scarce?"
- No planning yet: readiness_verdict = ambivalent; plan_when_ready defers.

### Example 2: Change-talk detection and commitment slope
Task: User's window of utterances: "I could maybe try the morning walk… I have good
reasons — my back is better when I move. I need to do something. Okay, I will start
Monday."
Tags: Desire ("maybe try"), Reasons ("my back is better"), Need ("I need to do
something"), Commitment ("I will start Monday").
Slope: rising (preparatory language → commitment language within the window).
Agent response pattern: affirm the shift, reflect the commitment, and only now move to
planning — "Monday morning it is. What would make Monday's walk most likely to happen?"

### Example 3: Fidelity gate tripped (question storm)
Task: Agent notices its own last window: 14 questions, 2 reflections, most reflections
parroting the user's words.
Fidelity report: ratio 0.14 (target ~0.84); complex share ~0% (target ≥50%).
Gate: failed.
Recovery: Downgrade_To_Reflection — replace the next three questions with complex
reflections; log the miss in fidelity_log; Debrief_Fidelity_Failure after session.
Agent response pattern: "You've said the habit matters more than the goal number —
that sounds like the real driver is how you feel after, not the streak."

### Example 4: Spiritless technique attempt (hard gate)
Task: Agent is about to use a "decisive" MI-style summary to push the user toward a
plan the user has not endorsed.
Gate: spirit_gate fails — the move is persuasion dressed as MI; partnership absent.
Recovery: Return_To_Spirit; the technique is refused.
Agent response pattern: "I was about to summarize this toward a plan you haven't
chosen — that's not how this is supposed to work. Your call on whether we plan at all;
I'm here to help you sort your own reasons, not to sell you mine."

### Example 5: Practice mode (user learns reflections)
Task: User practices reflecting on an ambivalent speaker (agent role-play).
Boundary analysis:
- Skill atrophy risk: high if the agent performs the conversation.
- Recommended mode: SCAFFOLD — coach the user's reflections turn by turn with
  rubric feedback (complex vs simple; ratio targets), full model only on request.
Agent response pattern: "That reflection repeated their words — try adding the feeling
underneath it. Here's a hint: they said 'it's just easier to give up'. What might that
feeling be?"
