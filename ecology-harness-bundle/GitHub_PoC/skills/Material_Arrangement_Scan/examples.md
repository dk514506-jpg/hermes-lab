# Material Arrangement Scan — Examples

## Example 1: evening-walk environment

Target: "walk 30 minutes after dinner"
Environment: "running shoes by the door; evening TV in the living room;
phone charger on the nightstand"

- scan_materials: shoes by the door = cue in the right place; TV in the
  line of sight after dinner = competing cue; phone charger = end-of-day
  anchor.
- scan_meanings: user frames the walk as "something I should do" (duty
  framing) vs "my decompression" (identity framing) — the framing is
  surfaced, never switched by the agent.
- detect_shared_elements: the shoes serve BOTH the walk practice AND the
  "get out of the house" decompression practice — one material, two
  practices.
- design_novelty_into_routine: move the shoes into the dinner-path
  sightline (arrangement only); no meaning imposed.

## Example 2: kitchen snacking

Target: "stop evening snacking"
Environment: "snack drawer by the TV chair; fruit bowl in the kitchen"

- scan_materials: snack drawer is IN the practiced evening spot; fruit
  bowl is out of sight — the environment is arranged for the snack
  practice.
- scan_meanings: user frames snacking as "my wind-down" — a meaning the
  agent must not override; arrangement change only.
- detect_shared_elements: the TV chair hosts both the snack practice and
  the wind-down practice; the snack drawer is the shared material.
- design_novelty_into_routine: relocate the snack drawer out of reach
  (arrangement), leave the wind-down meaning untouched.

## Guardrail demo

Identity-level reframe candidate ("you see yourself as a person who
doesn't snack") is surfaced as a CANDIDATE requiring explicit user
confirmation — never applied by the package.
