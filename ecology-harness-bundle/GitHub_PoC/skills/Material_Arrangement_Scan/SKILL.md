## Material Arrangement Scan

### Purpose
This skill applies the practice-theory lens (Shove, Pantzar & Watson 2012)
to why a routine sticks. A practice persists when its MATERIALS (physical/
digital environment), COMPETENCES (skills), and MEANINGS (framings) hold
together in a bundle. This package scans the user's environment through
that lens, detects shared elements across practice bundles, and proposes
arrangement changes — the environmental-design complement to COMB's
diagnosis and TDF's barrier grid.

Grounded in practice theory as carried in the estate's Theory_to_Routine_
Interface: materials/competences/meanings rows (T2R rows 94-96), the
bundle concept (row 116), and the governing rule "cues supplement, not
replace, practice" (row 94). The lens is DESCRIPTIVE: it explains why a
routine holds, it never dictates what the routine should be.

### Trigger Conditions
Use this skill when any of the following are true:
- The user describes their environment when discussing a stuck behavior
  ("the cookies are always on the counter", "my gym bag stays in the car").
- A COM-B diagnosis points at O-Ph (physical opportunity) or O-So (social
  opportunity) and the environment is the lever.
- A TDF barrier grid shows environmental/contextual barriers (domain 11).
- The user asks why a routine keeps happening despite intention — the
  arrangement answer.
- The user wants to make a behavior easier to start by changing their
  environment (cues, logistics, friction).

### Operation
1. scan_materials — inventory where the cue lives; propose rearrangements
   (the user rearranges; the agent never rearranges for them).
2. scan_meanings — surface the user's framing of the activity; meanings
   are user-owned; identity-level reframes require explicit confirmation
   (Phase 11 confirmed-Q2 path).
3. detect_shared_elements — find bundles: one competence or material
   serving two practices; the economy of rearrangement.
4. design_novelty_into_routine — propose arrangement changes that
   introduce novelty through arrangement, never by imposing meaning.

### Boundaries
- Cues supplement, never replace, practice (T2R row 94).
- Meaning-making is user-owned (Charter: preserve judgment); the agent
  surfaces framings, never imposes them.
- Identity-level reframes require explicit user confirmation before use.
- Proposals are suggestions — the user's arrangement authority is
  absolute.
