## 5. HONESTY CHECK

- **Does the coverage matrix overstate embedding? YES, for P2.** The one cell that makes
  "all 10 principles have at least one explicit mechanism" true (TDF-P2, line 29) is grounded in
  ops whose actual function is P4 (encode_version_metadata) and P9 (separate_evidence_
  interpretation) — the same ops already cited for P4 and P9. Striking it leaves P2 with no
  EMBEDDED cell, contradicting gap-analysis point 1 and the Verdict's "all 10 principles have
  real, named mechanisms in the 8 packages" (line 69-70). The FAOS mapping note (lines 75-80)
  partially rescues honesty by disclosing P2/P6 as interface-inherited — but then the verdict
  line should say 8-9/10 embedded + 2 FAOS-inherited, not "all 10."
- **Does the charter claim more than the artifacts deliver? Partly.** The Q0-Q10/Q0-Q5
  "cross-linked" guardrail (charter line 86-87) asserts a state the synthesis itself schedules as
  future work; the logics doc §5 transposition rows for state-lineage and absence-register claim
  estate instantiations that exist only as meld-doc mentions. The charter's interface rule
  (Q2.3), exemption (Q4.1), method-not-findings (Q4.5), and sovereignty (Q6.6) claims are all
  genuinely delivered — those hold.
- **Does the synthesis read smoother than the estate actually is? YES.** The synthesis
  "What the meld PROVES" section (lines 77-92) declares "The method was already embedded …
  the meld is a formalization, not a retrofit," while the matrix's own verdict is "HOLDS WITH
  GAPS" and P2/P6 live in FAOS, which is not yet integrated. "Already embedded" is true for
  P1/P3/P8/P10 and largely P4/P9/P7; it is not true for P2/P6. The synthesis also says the
  audit "verifies the 10 principles against the 8 packages" without the WITH-GAPS qualifier.
- **No overclaim found in:** corpus exemption, corpus freeze, mirror coherence (byte-identical),
  T2R count (48 entries — verified), lattice Q0-Q5 discipline, persona schema as prior-not-
  evidence, journal's honest "audit pending" status, verifier's own PASS banner (it does run
  what it claims to run — the gap is depth, not execution).

## 6. VALENS DISCIPLINE CHECK — does the meld violate the discipline it imports?

- **Premature coherence: minor violation.** The synthesis "PROVES" section and the logics-doc §5
  transposition rows both assert embedding/wiring that the estate does not yet have (P2/P6
  FAOS-inheritance, S0-S9 item lifecycle, absence-gating). This is precisely the imported P10
  ("do not synthesize yet") being violated in the meld's own self-description. The charter's
  guardrail text says the right thing; the synthesis and §5 table do not fully obey it.
- **Identity collapse (Valens Q0-Q10 vs lattice Q0-Q5): no collapse, but one near-miss.** The
  charter explicitly refuses unification, and lattice_index keeps Q0-Q5 as a separate tier set.
  The near-miss: logics doc §5 "Quarantine law (P8, Q0-Q10) → Lattice quarantine discipline"
  is a conflation-style mapping that only a single Q3_REJECTED note actually cross-links; the
  claimed "cross-linked" state (charter) is not yet delivered. Also the non-identity violation
  of asserting recovers_with direction as settled while T2R says AMBIGUOUS (W3) — shared names
  ("recovers_with") being treated as a resolved edge when both sides haven't agreed on
  direction is the exact failure the imported non-identity law names.
- **Findings-as-method: no violation.** Nothing in the meld imports astrological content as
  findings; the corpus stays outside and frozen; persona archetype is explicitly a framing prior,
  never evidence. Clean.
- **Untyped claims: isolated.** The "no-coercion" and "coder-level" matrix citations (W6) are
  ungrounded artifact references in a document whose method statement promises op/state names —
  an untyped-claim slip under the matrix's own P7-style discipline.
