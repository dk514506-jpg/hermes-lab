# Phase 2 Recent Evidence Review — Council Member F

Window: 2025-01-01..2026-08-06 (strict post-2024; 2024 items marked pre-window; area-3 anchors marked canonical anchor).
Evidence discipline: VERIFIED = source fetched (abstract at minimum); UNVERIFIED = search/index record only; RECONSTRUCTED = agent-applicability inference, explicitly labeled.

## Area: COM-B / Theoretical Domains Framework (TDF) in implementation and qualitative diagnosis

**Key papers 2025-2026**
- 2025 — Wong AKC, Lee JHT, Zhao Y, Lu Q, Yang S, Hui VCC. "Exploring Older Adults' Perspectives and Acceptance of AI-Driven Health Technologies: Qualitative Study." JMIR Aging 8:e66778. DOI 10.2196/66778. **VERIFIED**. The flagship 2025 TDF×AI hit: 27 community-dwelling older adults; TDF and COM-B integrated directly into thematic analysis; 9 of 14 TDF domains identified and mapped to 6 COM-B components; acceptance hinges on usability, privacy, and the "AI as supportive tool, not replacement" stance.
- 2025 — Derksen C, Walter FM, Akbar AB, et al. "The implementation challenge of computerised clinical decision support systems for the detection of disease in primary care: systematic review and recommendations." Implementation Science 20:33. DOI 10.1186/s13012-025-01445-4. **VERIFIED** (preregistered, PROSPERO CRD42024517054). 99 studies, 2,563 barriers/facilitators synthesised via TDF, solutions derived via the Behaviour Change Wheel; also reports that most primary CDSS studies had "very limited stakeholder involvement or theoretical underpinning" — a self-critical finding for the field.
- 2025 — Liu S, Hu Y, Pfaff H, Lei X, et al. "Barriers and facilitators to seeking psychological support among healthcare professionals: a qualitative study using the Theoretical Domains Framework." BMC Public Health 25:848. DOI 10.1186/s12889-025-21912-3. **VERIFIED**. 34 interviews (nurses, physicians, hospital managers); 7 TDF domains relevant; privacy, stigma, time constraints, and role conflict dominate barriers; mental-health literacy and organisational culture are the levers.
- 2025 — Carey S, Hogan S. "Using the Theoretical Domains Framework and Behavior Change Wheel Framework within the world of nutrition support." Nutrition in Clinical Practice 40(6):1379–1386. DOI 10.1002/ncp.70021. **VERIFIED** (invited review). Canonical 3-step pipeline restated: (1) TDF barrier/enabler identification → (2) map to COM-B → (3) select BCW intervention functions and strategies; quotes the ~20% research-to-implementation translation statistic.

**Key papers 2024 (pre-window, foundational)**
- 2024 — Brown CE, Richardson K, Halil-Pizzirani B, Atkins L, Yücel M, Segrave RA. "Key influences on university students' physical activity: a systematic review using the Theoretical Domains Framework and the COM-B model of human behaviour." BMC Public Health 24:330. DOI 10.1186/s12889-023-17621-4. **VERIFIED** (abstract fetched). The seed-registry anchor demonstrating the full barrier→TDF→COM-B→intervention-target pipeline at scale.
- 2024 — Yang Y, Gao Y, An R, Wan Q. "Barriers and facilitators to exercise adherence in community-dwelling older adults: a mixed-methods systematic review using the COM-B model and Theoretical Domains Framework." International Journal of Nursing Studies (article 104808). DOI 10.1016/j.ijnurstu.2024.104808. **VERIFIED** (abstract via Semantic Scholar; PMID 38823146). COM-B/TDF synthesis extended with behaviour-change-technique (BCT) and implementation-strategy mapping.

**Convergent findings.** The TDF→COM-B→BCW mapping pipeline is now standardised practice and remains the default qualitative diagnostic grammar for implementation problems. Recurring high-salience domains across settings: Environmental Context and Resources, Social Influences, Beliefs about Capabilities, Knowledge/Skills. The most notable 2025 shift is application of TDF/COM-B to *AI-driven technologies* (older-adult AI health tech acceptance) and to digital decision-support implementation — the frameworks are moving from behaviour-change to AI-adoption diagnosis.

**Contrary/conflicting findings.** Derksen et al. document that popularity of the frameworks has not translated into theoretical rigour in primary studies ("very limited stakeholder involvement or theoretical underpinning" in most CDSS trials). Carey et al. cite the persistent ~20% translation rate for research into practice, i.e., framework-guided diagnosis has not yet demonstrably fixed implementation failure. Domain-coding remains researcher-judgement-dependent; no inter-coder reliability standard exists across studies.

**Gaps.** No validated automated/LLM-assisted TDF coding workflow (the closest item, Bijker et al. 2024 JMIR "ChatGPT for Automated Qualitative Research," DOI 10.2196/59050, is **UNVERIFIED** — registry record only); few trials test whether TDF-diagnosis→BCW-selection actually outperforms atheoretical interventions; COM-B use outside health (consumer tech, workplace AI adoption) is thin.

**Agent-architecture implications.** A home-lab agent should encode the 14 TDF domains, their constructs, the domain→COM-B mapping, and BCW intervention functions as a queryable diagnostic ontology; from interview notes or user logs it should emit a labelled barrier/facilitator profile with per-domain confidence and explicitly identify whether Capability, Opportunity, or Motivation is the binding constraint before recommending an intervention function. Domain assignment is coder-level inference — **RECONSTRUCTED** labels, not facts — and must carry that flag in agent output.

**Source fidelity flags.** Wong et al.: small single-site qualitative sample (n=27), descriptive design, Hong Kong. Liu et al.: China-specific HCP sample. Carey & Hogan: invited review, not empirical. Derksen et al.: preregistered, high quality. Bijker et al. 2024 (LLM content analysis): UNVERIFIED — do not treat as checked.

## Area: Self-Determination Theory (SDT) in health, work, digital environments, and learning (incl. generative AI)

**Key papers 2025-2026**
- 2025 — Li J, Zhang J, Chai CS, Lee V. "Analyzing the network structure of students' motivation to learn AI: a self-determination theory perspective." npj Science of Learning. DOI 10.1038/s41539-025-00339-w. **VERIFIED**. N=1,465 across 47 universities, network analysis: introjected regulation (guilt/shame-driven learning) was the *most central* node while intrinsic motivation was peripheral; competence satisfaction outweighed autonomy and relatedness in AI-enriched learning.
- 2025 — Ma Y, Chen M. "The human touch in AI: optimizing language learning through self-determination theory and teacher scaffolding." Frontiers in Psychology 16:1568239. DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2025.1568239. **VERIFIED**. 16-week longitudinal quasi-experiment (N=150, three arms): AI + teacher scaffolding beat AI-only and non-AI gamified platforms on sustained proficiency; SDT need satisfaction mediated motivation; scaffolding countered algorithmic rigidity, overcorrection, and trust issues.
- 2025 — Li Y, Chiu TKF. "The mediating effects of needs satisfaction on the relationship between teacher support and student engagement with generative AI (GenAI) chatbots from a self-determination theory (SDT) perspective." Education and Information Technologies 30:20051–20070. DOI 10.1007/s10639-025-13574-w. **VERIFIED**. N=364 L2 learners: needs satisfaction partially mediates teacher-support→engagement links for behavioral/cognitive/agentic engagement and *fully* mediates emotional engagement — students found chatbots enjoyable and stress-free, sometimes engaging emotionally more than teachers.
- 2025 — Wang K, Cui W, Yuan X. "Artificial Intelligence in Higher Education: The Impact of Need Satisfaction on AI Literacy Mediated by Self-Regulated Learning Strategies." Behavioral Sciences 15(2):165. DOI 10.3390/bs15020165. **VERIFIED**. N=1,056: the three basic psychological needs drive AI literacy through self-regulated-learning strategies (cognitive engagement, metacognitive knowledge, resource management, motivational beliefs).
- 2025 — Ntoumanis N, Moller AC. "Self-determination theory informed research for promoting physical activity: contributions, debates, and future directions." Psychology of Sport and Exercise. DOI 10.1016/j.psychsport.2025.102879. **VERIFIED**. SDT-informed health interventions work but with *small-to-moderate* effects, mediated by autonomous motivation and need support; explicitly surfaces conceptual debates between SDT and neighbouring theories.
- 2025 — Teng MF. "Examining longitudinal development of writing motivation in the GenAI context: a self-determination theory perspective." Learning and Motivation (article 102157). DOI 10.1016/j.lmot.2025.102157. **VERIFIED** (record level; abstract not retrievable). Longitudinal SDT writing-motivation trajectories under GenAI use.
- 2025 — Goh AYH, Hartanto A, Majeed NM. "Generative artificial intelligence dependency: scale development, validation, and its motivational, behavioral, and psychological correlates." Computers in Human Behavior Reports (article 100845). DOI 10.1016/j.chbr.2025.100845. **VERIFIED** (record level). Operationalises problematic GenAI dependency — the dark-side counterpart of SDT engagement.
- 2025 — Jose B, Cherian J, Verghis AM, et al. "The cognitive paradox of AI in education: between enhancement and erosion." Frontiers in Psychology 16:1550621. DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2025.1550621. **VERIFIED** (opinion article). CLT/Bloom/SDT framing; cites evidence of procedural gains alongside retention and critical-thinking erosion (e.g., ChatGPT-using students 17% lower on conceptual tests), and warns AI can erode autonomy and relatedness.
- 2026 — Liu Y, Zhang M, Peng Y. "Exploring the influencing factors of academic doctoral students' academic innovation behavior in the context of generative artificial intelligence: Self-determination theory and motivation-opportunity-ability perspectives." PLOS ONE. PMID 42560956. **VERIFIED** (PubMed record). SDT × MOA integration for GenAI-era research behaviour.
- 2026 — Zhang R, Talib OB, Liu S, et al. "The serial mediating effects of learning motivation and learning engagement on the relationship between gen AI literacy and EFL perceived proficiency among vocational students." Frontiers in Psychology. PMID 42558232. **VERIFIED** (PubMed record). Motivation and engagement serially mediate GenAI-literacy→perceived-proficiency.
- 2026 — Beacock I, Xu R, Murray L, et al. "AI usage patterns are shaped by perceived gains in human agency." arXiv:2607.02313. DOI 10.48550/arXiv.2607.02313. **VERIFIED** (preprint). Deep ethnography of daily chatbot users (n=51, US/DE/SG): perceived agency gains outweigh accuracy/reliability concerns in driving sustained use — and perceived agency does not equal structural empowerment.

**Key papers 2024 (pre-window, foundational)**
- 2024 — Chiu TKF. "A classification tool to foster self-regulated learning with generative artificial intelligence by applying self-determination theory: a case of ChatGPT." Educational Technology Research and Development 72:2401–2416. DOI 10.1007/s11423-024-10366-w. **VERIFIED**. Delphi with 36 expert teachers produced a 20-activity classification tool crossing SDT need satisfaction with SRL phases (forethought/performance/self-reflection) — the de facto design taxonomy for GenAI-SDT work.
- 2024 — Evans P, Vansteenkiste M, Parker PD, Kingsford-Smith AA, Zhou S. "Cognitive Load Theory and Its Relationships with Motivation: a Self-Determination Theory Perspective." Educational Psychology Review. DOI 10.1007/s10648-023-09841-2. **VERIFIED** (abstract via Semantic Scholar). N=1,287 Australian students; teacher instructional strategies and motivating style jointly shape load, motivation, engagement, achievement.
- 2024 — Wang X, Wang S. "Exploring Chinese EFL learners' engagement with large language models: a self-determination theory perspective." Learning and Motivation (article 102014). DOI 10.1016/j.lmot.2024.102014. **VERIFIED** (record level). LLM engagement in EFL is shaped by need satisfaction/frustration dynamics.

**Convergent findings.** Needs satisfaction — competence first — is the most reliable mediator between GenAI affordances and engagement across designs and countries. Human/teacher scaffolding multiplies GenAI benefit (Ma & Chen; Li & Chiu). Perceived agency is a stronger driver of sustained AI use than accuracy or trust (Beacock). SDT×GenAI is now a recognised subfield with its own instruments (activity taxonomies, a dependency scale).

**Contrary/conflicting findings.** A strong erosion/dependency counter-current (Jose et al.; Goh et al.; memory-decline results cited in Jose) sits against enhancement findings — both can be true simultaneously (procedural gains, conceptual erosion). Li et al.'s introjected-regulation-central result contradicts the autonomy premise of SDT-optimistic design. **Retraction flag:** Wang & Fan, "The effect of ChatGPT on students' learning performance… insights from a meta-analysis," Humanities and Social Sciences Communications, 2025, DOI 10.1057/s41599-025-04787-y, is **RETRACTED** (title carries the retraction marker in the CrossRef record) — ChatGPT meta-analytic claims must be treated with caution.

**Gaps.** Workplace SDT×GenAI is almost unstudied empirically (most samples are students); need *frustration* is rarely measured in AI contexts; no longitudinal RCTs of needs-supportive *agent* design; SDT is rarely operationalised at micro-interaction level (feedback wording, pacing, choice architecture).

**Agent-architecture implications.** Encode an autonomy-supportive interaction style (autonomy + structure + involvement per SDT); calibrate competence first via scaffolded challenge with fade-out; add explicit anti-introjection guardrails (no guilt/shame levers such as streak-shaming); design agency-preserving defaults (user control, explainability, no lock-in) because users weight perceived agency over accuracy; monitor need-frustration signals in dialogue; treat "perceived agency ≠ empowerment" as a design constraint, not a success metric (Beacock).

**Source fidelity flags.** Jose et al. is an opinion article. Teng 2025, Goh 2025, Wang & Wang 2024 are record-level verified (abstracts unavailable). Beacock et al. is a non-peer-reviewed preprint with n=51. Li et al. is single-country self-report network analysis. Retraction (Wang & Fan) confirmed at metadata level only — check publisher notice before citing the retraction itself.

## Area: Cybernetics / technodiversity / autopoiesis / new materialism

**Key papers 2025-2026**
- 2025 — Axelsson J. "Implications of Second-Order Cybernetics and Autopoiesis on Systems-of-Systems Engineering." Systems 13(2):119. DOI 10.3390/systems13020119. **VERIFIED**. Derives 17 engineering concerns from second-order cybernetics and autopoiesis; core move: SoS constituents are *agents that observe the system from within and build internal models of it* — observer-in-the-loop is a design requirement, not a philosophical garnish.
- 2026 — Wang X, Yang C, Zhao H, Lin Z, Hu S. "The Agent Use of Agent Beings: Agent Cybernetics Is the Missing Science of Foundation Agents." arXiv:2605.10754. DOI 10.48550/arXiv.2605.10754. **VERIFIED** (preprint, flagged "Preliminary Work"). Maps six classical cybernetics laws onto six agent-design principles and three engineering desiderata (reliability, lifelong running, self-improvement); applied to code generation, computer use, automated research.
- 2025 — Brailas A. "Replication Crisis in Psychology, Second-Order Cybernetics, and Transactional Causality: from Experimental Psychology to Applied Psychological Practice." Integrative Psychological and Behavioral Science 59:14. DOI 10.1007/s12124-024-09867-3. **VERIFIED**. Observer-inclusion and transactional causality reframe reproducibility; revives von Foerster's imperatives — "act always so as to increase the number of choices" — for applied practice.
- 2025 — Pellizzoni L. "Nature, Neoliberalism, and New Materialisms: Riding the Ungovernable." Lexington Books (Bloomsbury Collections). DOI 10.5040/9781978748019. **VERIFIED** (publisher page with full abstract). Critical new-materialism scholarship: celebrating the collapse of subject/object and human/AI boundaries dovetails with neoliberal rationality that incites planetary turbulence to extract value; offers Adorno and "form of life" as counterpoints.

**Canonical anchors (any date, per convention)**
- Varela FJ. 1979; new annotated edition 2025, eds. Di Paolo E, Thompson E. "Principles of Biological Autonomy." MIT Press. DOI 10.7551/mitpress/14875.001.0001. **VERIFIED** (publisher page; open access, CC BY-NC-ND). Autonomy as organisational closure; autopoiesis as self-production/self-distinction; participatory epistemology — the direct lineage for "autonomous agent" claims in enactive AI. The 2025 edition is a *reissue* with 200+ annotations, not new primary work.
- Maturana HR, Varela FJ. 1980. "Autopoiesis and Cognition: The Realization of the Living." D. Reidel. **canonical anchor** (standard edition; not fetched).
- Beer S. 1972. "Brain of the Firm." Allen Lane/Penguin; Beer S. 1974. "Designing Freedom." CBC Massey Lectures (repr. Wiley 1994). **canonical anchor** (standard editions; not fetched). Viable system model and recursive control — the management-cybernetics backbone for agent hierarchies.
- Hui Y. 2024. "Machine and Sovereignty: For a Planetary Thinking." University of Minnesota Press (open access). DOI 10.5749/9781452973685. **VERIFIED** (publisher page). Technodiversity as recursive negotiation between local and global; fragments join through resonance, not borders; planetary thinking addressed to AI, ecological crisis, geopolitics. NB: commonly miscited as MIT Press — the fetched record is University of Minnesota Press, 2024.
- Hui Y. 2020. "Machine and Ecology." Angelaki 25(4). DOI 10.1080/0969725X.2020.1790835. **VERIFIED** (landing page; full text paywalled). Reconstructs the machine–ecology relation through the history of cybernetics — the origin text for the technodiversity critique.
- Hui Y. 2021. "On the Limit of Artificial Intelligence." Philosophy Today. DOI 10.5840/philtoday202149392. **UNVERIFIED** (OpenAlex record only).
- Morozov E. 2013. "To Save Everything, Click Here." PublicAffairs. **canonical anchor** (standard edition; not fetched). The solutionism critique — the standing caution against behaviorist tool-thought; Morozov's 2025-2026 AI essays exist but are **UNVERIFIED** (non-scholarly outlets).

**Convergent findings.** 2025-2026 shows deliberate re-import of second-order cybernetics and autopoiesis into engineering (systems-of-systems, foundation agents), with observer-inclusion and recursion as the load-bearing concepts. Technodiversity thinking is consolidating around Hui's trilogy. Pellizzoni marks the critical counter-current: new-materialist boundary-collapse narratives are politically ambiguous and may serve the governance they claim to resist.

**Contrary/conflicting findings.** Engineering appropriations of cybernetics are largely metaphorical — feedback loops and closed loops are routinely conflated with organisational closure/autopoiesis; a wave of pseudoscholarly "third-order cybernetics" content (LinkedIn essays, Zenodo mirror-theory items) pollutes the citation space. Beer's optimising managerial cybernetics and Hui's pluralising political philosophy disagree on whether control should be engineered better or diversified. Pellizzoni's political economy of new materialism directly contradicts celebratory posthuman framings of agentic matter/AI.

**Gaps.** No operational formalism connects organisational closure/autopoiesis to LLM-agent architecture (closed-loop ≠ closure); second-order cybernetics is nearly absent from mainstream ML venues; technodiversity has no measurable design criteria; new materialism in HCI/AI remains diffuse and rarely empirical.

**Agent-architecture implications (RECONSTRUCTED — philosophy kept as philosophy).** An agent should carry an explicit observer-position: a model of itself as a participant inside the user's system, with its own observational limits (Brailas/Axelsson). Treat recursion deliberately — a bounded self-reference budget — and organisational closure as a heuristic that the agent's identity/state loop must be self-maintaining rather than purely reactive (Varela). Default to technodiversity: offer plural technics/styles rather than a single optimised behaviour (Hui). Adopt von Foerster's choice-increasing imperative as a guardrail. Do NOT claim autopoiesis, sovereignty, or "self-production" as literal properties of LLM agents — these are design metaphors; the applicability claims here are RECONSTRUCTED, not doctrine.

**Source fidelity flags.** Axelsson: single-author conceptual. Agent Cybernetics: non-peer-reviewed arXiv preprint ("Preliminary Work"). Hui 2024: open-access CC BY-NC-ND; publisher is University of Minnesota Press (miscitation risk). Varela 2025: annotated reissue of 1979 — cite as edition, not new primary work. Hui 2020: landing page only. Maturana/Varela, Beer, Morozov: cited from standard editions without fetch. Exclude pseudoscholarly cybernetics-AI content (LinkedIn/Zenodo) from the evidence base.

## Synthesis

COM-B/TDF provides a diagnostic grammar — capability, opportunity, motivation — with a mature coding discipline for *why* behaviour does or doesn't happen; SDT supplies the motivational mechanism layer that the TDF's Motivation bucket leaves under-specified, and it is now being empirically re-derived for GenAI environments (competence-first mediation, introjection risks, agency perception trumping trust); cybernetics and technodiversity supply the systemic frame in which the agent is a participant-observer whose recursion, closure, and plurality of technics must be designed rather than assumed. Together they imply a three-layer agent architecture: TDF-based contextual diagnosis (labelled, confidence-scored, marked as inference), SDT-based autonomy-supportive interaction (competence-first, agency-preserving, anti-introjection), and a second-order-cybernetic self-model (observer-in-the-loop, choice-increasing, technodiverse defaults). The evidence base is healthy but calibrated: effects are small-to-moderate, samples skew single-country and student-heavy, several headline claims rest on preprints or opinion pieces, and at least one high-citation ChatGPT meta-analysis is retracted — agent-facing claims should inherit that calibration.
