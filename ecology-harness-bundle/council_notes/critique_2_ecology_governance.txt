## 2. The limitations (what is weak or wrong — specific)

- **The campaign failed to register itself in the ecology it governs.** 03_Open_Questions_Register.txt and 05_Project_Atlas.txt carry timestamps identical to seed creation (17:34); the Foundation campaign (18:23) never touched them. The register still holds its 5 seed questions — none of the campaign's live questions (TDF 12-vs-14 operationalization, reward-undermining vs. reward design in Proximal_Practice_Selector, the OneDrive write-path constraint, the 17-26% prediction ceiling) got QIDs, "Last Witnessed" dates, or Right of Return entries. The Atlas does not even list "Motivational Ecology" as an active project. The charter's "active project relationships rather than isolated archives" is violated at the top level.
- **No journal, no handoff record.** Plan §7.5 specifies handoff_notes.md; zero files matching journal/handoff/continuity exist in the Ecology tree. Continuity currently lives in the parent's context window — precisely the "stored" rather than "enacted" continuity the charter forbids (Charter line 13). Interruption today means re-deriving the campaign.
- **A status-line category error.** README stamps "Phases 1-2 VERIFIED (assembled from council distillations)." VERIFIED means source-fetched; a parent-side synthesis of subagent distillations is a reconstruction. Under the corpus's own printed-vs-reconstructed and ordinal≠completed rules, the syntheses should read RECONSTRUCTED-assembled, with VERIFIED reserved for sources. The same bleed recurs at the skill layer: Human_Empowerment_Boundary cites Beacock 2026 (preprint), ProACT (preprint), Xu 2026, Liu 2026 as unmarked grounding — the evidence files carry status flags, the operational skills that cite them do not. The separation stops at the archive door.
- **Sovereignty encoded but never enacted.** No user verdict record exists anywhere; the campaign ran fully agent-side in one day (all files 17:32-18:23, 2026-08-06). Dallas's revision/rejection/quarantine rights are protocol, never exercised. Encoding without enactment is one interruption away from stored identity.
- **The self-application gap — sharpest limitation.** The architecture's headline principle ("skill preservation is a measured objective, not a slogan," Contrary_Findings D2) is not applied to its own user. Councils of subagents did Dallas's reading, distilling, and judging. No skill_load_metric tracks whether Dallas's own Valens-reading and critique capability is rising or falling as Pip does the work. The campaign describes the architecture it does not embody — it is an instance of the very over-assistance pattern its Layer 4 exists to prevent.
