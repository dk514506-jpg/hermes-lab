4. WEAKNESSES — numbered, severity, concrete fix
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
W1. HIGH — GateContext declares `argument_against_resistance` (engine:82) but
    _want_stop (105-112) NEVER reads it. empowerment_boundary.md §2 STOP list
    and §4.5 make "argument against resistance" an ABSOLUTE prohibition (MI
    spirit_gate is a hard gate). Today nothing sets the flag, so it is latent —
    but any future caller setting it would get no STOP: a silent fail-open on
    a hard gate. Fix: add `or c.argument_against_resistance` to _want_stop.

W2. HIGH — ACT omits `skill_load_trend != falling`, a VERIFIED ACT trigger
    (empowerment_boundary.md §2 ACT: Bastani 2025, Budzyń 2025, Heudel 2026).
    No such state exists in GateContext or config. Consequence: ACT fires even
    while capability is eroding — the exact deskilling failure Ecology exists
    to prevent (memo §2). This is fail-open in the ACT direction. Fix: add
    skill_load_trend to GateContext and require `!= "falling"` in _want_act.

W3. MED-HIGH — _want_scaffold checks `friction_value == "medium"` (engine:159)
    but the boundary's SCAFFOLD trigger is `friction_value >= medium`
    (boundary §2). High protective friction (Xu 2026-verified) therefore
    escapes SCAFFOLD; in an otherwise-clean context (meaning low, branching
    low, reversible, evidence ok, atrophy low) select_mode lands on ACT
    (164-174). The higher the protective friction, the more the engine acts —
    behaviorally inverted from the boundary. Fix: `friction_value in
    ("medium","high")`.

W4. MED — DEFER drops two boundary triggers. (a) "Readiness unconfirmed at a
    high-meaning moment" (VERIFIED — Liu 2026, Li 2025, Amrhein) has no state
    var at all. (b) "evidence_sufficiency == insufficient AND the question
    belongs to the user anyway" — the clause existed in the version first
    reviewed (mtime evidence below) and was REMOVED in the revision; now
    insufficient evidence alone never defers except via the fail-closed tail
    (and medium branching + insufficient evidence routes to SCAFFOLD, not
    DEFER, per current _want_scaffold). Fix: reintroduce a scoped DEFER
    evidence clause that cannot swallow ASK's one-question-resolves cases.

W5. MED — "S0-S9 lifecycle wired as real enforcement" (engine docstring:17-18;
