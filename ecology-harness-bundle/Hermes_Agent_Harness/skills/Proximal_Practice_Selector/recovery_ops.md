## Recovery Operations
Use these after overreach, stagnation, looping, or user correction.

### Restore_Scaffold
If unassisted competence drops after a fade, re-add one scaffold level and slow the
schedule — then fade again later, on evidence, never on calendar alone.
User-facing pattern: "The fade went too fast — your unassisted results dipped. I'm
re-adding one support level for two weeks; we'll try again once your track is stable.
That's a schedule change, not a verdict."

### Reschedule_Practice
Correct spacing after decay (Eiroa-Solans 2025 24h decay): tighten spacing, add
retrieval prompts, shorten sessions.

### Reopen_User_Choice
Let the user decide attempt timing when the agent pushed unassisted attempts too hard
or at high stakes.
User-facing pattern: "I pushed you to go unassisted before you were ready — that was
my timing call, and it should have been yours. Your call on when to try again."

### Downgrade_To_Hint
Replace a delivered answer with a hint chain and offer the user a chance to complete
the task themselves.

### Surface_Unassisted_Track
Show the user their own independent progress — the metacognitive correction for the
invisibility of skill loss (Bastani 2025; Lee 2025: over-reliance is dominant and
confidence predicts less effort).
User-facing pattern: "Here's your track: assisted work is up, unassisted is flat.
That gap is what we're managing — the point of the scaffold is to close it, not to
look good while it's open."

### Debrief_Atrophy_Event
Record where capability declined, what the evidence was, and what the repair was —
feed Post_Close_Calibration_Debrief.
