## Support Operations
Use these when prerequisites are missing before the lattice can proceed.

### Separate_Evidence_Interpretation_Implication
Rebuild any claim into disciplined layers (observation | interpretation | implication |
action) when overinterpretation risk appears. This is the lattice's invariant: every
node must be layer-tagged before it may enter the lattice.

### Mark_As_Provisional
When any insight is used outside the lattice, attach: hypothesis status, supporting
observation citations, evidence sufficiency, and confidence. Never emit an insight
without this envelope.

### Cite_Supporting_Observations
When an insight lacks citations, refuse to promote it. Collect the raw observations
first (capture_observation), then re-form the interpretation from the evidence.

### Open_Observation_Gap
When evidence sufficiency is insufficient, ask the user a targeted question or request
one more artifact/reflection before forming an interpretation. Do not fabricate
evidence to close the gap.

### Quarantine_Insight
Move an identity-level or low-evidence insight into the quarantined set. Quarantined
insights may not steer action, may not appear as facts, and are visible to the user
(with reasons) in quarantined_insights.md.

### Ask_Targeted_Clarification
When a single ambiguity blocks lattice construction (e.g., whether an observation was
self-initiated or imposed), ask one precise question. Do not guess the answer.

### Request_Observation_Consent
When the material to be latticed was not explicitly opted in, stop and ask. Latticing
is bounded to agreed sources; practice logs never become surveillance.
