# Feedback Ecology Map — Examples

## Example 1: stalled evening walk

Target: "walk 30 minutes after dinner"
Statements: "I did it three nights. Then I just... didn't. It made sense
at the time but it faded."

- assess_coherence: coherence meaning = "my decompression" (user-defined);
  differentiation = walk vs other wind-downs; internalization = LOW (the
  routine never became "part of me" — user's own words).
- coherence_questions (agent asks, never supplies): "What made the walk
  feel like yours those three nights?" / "What changed when it faded?"
- form_cmo_hypothesis: Context (dinner schedule shifted) + Mechanism
  (coherence — internalization stalled) → Outcome (routine faded after 3
  nights). Hypothesis status: hypothesis, feeds calibration.

## Example 2: well-embedded habit

Target: "morning journal"
Statements: "It's just what I do now. I'd feel off without it."

- assess_coherence: internalization = HIGH (user's own framing: "just
  what I do"); meaning = "clearing my head" (user-defined).
- form_cmo_hypothesis: Context (stable morning routine) + Mechanism
  (coherence — internalized) → Outcome (habit persists without prompting).
  The hypothesis explains WHY it sticks — usable for other routines, not
  a verdict on this one.

## Guardrail demo

If the agent were to frame the walk as "you're someone who abandons
routines," that would be an IMPOSED meaning — the package instead asks
what the user's framing is. Identity-level framings require explicit
confirmation (Phase 11 confirmed-Q2 path).
