## Purpose

The systematic counter-evidence file: findings that qualify, contradict, or
bound the main-line evidence; critiques of the frameworks themselves; and the
limits of what the post-2024 literature can support. Valens-style: contrary
findings are preserved, not harmonized.

## A. Framework-Level Critiques

### A1. COM-B / BCW / BCT

- **Ogden (2016, Health Psychology Review, VERIFIED abstract):** COM-B/BCW systematisation "may be neither feasible nor desirable" — the protocol-coding→behaviour translational chain is gappy; over-standardisation reduces practitioners to "technicians." Published rebuttal debate exists (Teixeira 2016; Peters & Kok 2016; Abraham 2016 — titles VERIFIED, contents unverified).
- **Descriptive, not predictive (VERIFIED, Michie 2011's own contrast):** COM-B cannot say which component will matter most; function selection depends on judgment; mapping matrices theory-derived, not empirically calibrated.
- **Mechanism links are an open programme (VERIFIED):** BCTv1 explicitly does not unpack which BCT works through which mechanism (later Mechanisms of Action project). BCT coding captures content, not dose, quality, fidelity, or delivery skill.
- **Halonen (2024, non-peer-reviewed commentary, flagged UNVERIFIED-quality):** BCW "too broad," lacks predictive specificity and comparative validation. Use with caution — not peer-reviewed.
- **Willmott 2021 sample caveat (RECONSTRUCTED):** ~80% female young adults; generalization limited.

### A2. TDF / Implementation Science

- **Cane 2012's own validation data are weak by its stated thresholds (VERIFIED):** average silhouette 0.29 below the 0.50 "weak structure" cutoff; Knowledge did not form an independent cluster; Environmental Context/Resources, Behavioural Regulation, Memory/Attention/Decision clustered weakly, retained only on closed-sort confidence.
- **12-domain vs 14-domain witness conflict (VERIFIED, preserved not harmonized):** Michie 2005 (12 domains) vs Cane 2012 (14). Cane frames itself as "an advance"; an agent must encode version as metadata. Secondary discrepancy: Cane says Michie 2005 involved 16 researchers; PubMed abstract says 13.
- **TDF is a framework, not a process theory (RECONSTRUCTED):** specifies no relations among domains.
- **NPT formalism vs process fluidity (VERIFIED, May & Finch's own caveat):** the tabular propositional form implies "a degree of rigidity that is completely alien to the phenomena."
- **NPT power under-theorised (RECONSTRUCTED reading of VERIFIED text):** May & Finch acknowledge "the play of power" but do not theorize it.
- **Realist mechanisms unobservable by definition (VERIFIED via TASO/secondaries):** falsification ambiguous; mechanism/context boundary disputes (Lacouture 2015, UNVERIFIED).
- **Framework popularity ≠ theoretical rigour (VERIFIED, Derksen 2025):** most primary CDSS studies had "very limited stakeholder involvement or theoretical underpinning"; the ~20% research-to-practice translation rate persists (Carey & Hogan 2025, VERIFIED).

### A3. SDT

- **Need satisfaction vs need frustration as distinct processes (VERIFIED, Vansteenkiste, Ryan & Soenens 2020):** frustration predicts ill-being independently; not opposite-pole.
- **Universality vs cultural variability (VERIFIED as debated).** Needs-list extension proposals (VERIFIED as discussed).
- **Measurement critiques (VERIFIED):** self-report scales; "needs" definitional criteria.
- **Modest, heterogeneous effects (VERIFIED, Ntoumanis 2021 meta):** health-domain effect sizes small-to-moderate.
- **Reward-undermining controversy (VERIFIED):** Deci, Koestner & Ryan 1999 meta vs Eisenberger & Cameron 1996 — the standing empirical dispute; the 1999 meta prevails in the field but the dispute is preserved.
- **Introjection can dominate in GenAI learning (VERIFIED, Li 2025 npj):** introjected regulation most central node — complicates SDT-optimistic design assumptions.

### A4. MI

- **Null trials and site-by-site variability (VERIFIED, Miller & Rose 2009 "Mixed Findings"):** eating disorders, smoking, some drug trials; multisite variability.
- **Workshop-only training fails to change practice (VERIFIED); self-reported proficiency unrelated to coded proficiency (VERIFIED, Miller & Mount 2001).**
- **MI without spirit is not MI (VERIFIED, Kuchipudi et al. 1990):** technical manipulation without partnership shows no effect — the architectural red line.
- **Frequency-vs-strength measurement issue (VERIFIED, Amrhein 2003):** commitment *slope* predicts outcomes, not frequency/intercept.
- **Relational vs technical components unresolved (VERIFIED):** Sellman 2001 supports technical; Karno & Longabaugh 2005 reactance interaction.
- **Disingenuous change talk (VERIFIED as discussed).** Effect-size debates outside substance use.
- **AI-delivered MI: perceived empathy consistently lags humans (VERIFIED, MIBot); primary outcomes fail while readiness succeeds (VERIFIED, Li 2025); automatic fidelity coding ~52% accurate (VERIFIED, Han 2026).**
- **Bahri 2025 psychiatric-effects critique (UNVERIFIED, search snippet only).**

### A5. Practice Theory / Cybernetics

- **Practice theory has no established intervention evidence base (VERIFIED, Welch 2017):** "efficacy has yet to be empirically established." Definitional heterogeneity across lineages (VERIFIED, Welch note i). Shove's three-element model vs Schatzki's practices + material-arrangements distinction (VERIFIED, Welch note iii).
- **Individual-agency critique (RECONSTRUCTED via footnote):** some Schatzki readings have individualistic implications (Welch & Warde 2017) — the anti-individualist stance is not uniform.
- **Cybernetics engineering appropriations are largely metaphorical (VERIFIED synthesis):** feedback loops and closed loops routinely conflated with organisational closure/autopoiesis. Closed-loop ≠ closure — the agent must not claim autopoiesis as a literal property.
- **Pseudoscholarly pollution (VERIFIED):** "third-order cybernetics" LinkedIn essays, Zenodo mirror-theory items — excluded from the evidence base.
- **Beer vs Hui disagree (RECONSTRUCTED):** engineer control better vs diversify technics.
- **Pellizzoni's political economy critique (VERIFIED):** new-materialist boundary-collapse narratives may serve the governance they claim to resist.

## B. Evidence-Level Contrary Findings (2024-2026)

1. **Productivity vs capability (VERIFIED, both):** Brynjolfsson 2025 (+14% productivity, no measured skill loss) vs Bastani 2025 (−17% post-access capability). Not contradictory — performance and capability are different measures — but almost no study measures both; the architecture must.
2. **Perceived agency vs actual capacity (VERIFIED, Beacock 2026):** adoption driven by perceived agency that can be illusory ("empowerment theater").
3. **Enhancement vs erosion in GenAI learning (VERIFIED, Jose 2025 opinion + empirical):** procedural gains and conceptual erosion co-occur (17% lower conceptual tests).
4. **Personalization can hurt (VERIFIED, Ma 2025):** persona-primed LLMs performed below random chance with demographic info; unprimed baselines better.
5. **Prediction ceilings (VERIFIED):** next-action alignment only 17.1-26% (Shaikh 2026); proactive success 26-40% (ProEvent, PROBE); automatic MI coding 52.6% (Han 2026).
6. **AI-MI readiness without behavior (VERIFIED):** 3/15 studies with substantial behavior change (Karve 2025); 24h decay (Eiroa-Solans 2025); hesitancy unchanged (Li 2025).
7. **Attribution of proactive failure is contested (VERIFIED):** UniClawBench finds model+framework jointly matter; PROBE attributes most variance to model.
8. **VR training modality limits empathy (VERIFIED, Verhoeven 2025):** only 40% empathy with virtual patients.
9. **Derksen 2025 self-critique (VERIFIED):** the frameworks' own field lacks theoretical rigour in application.
10. **Ntoumanis 2025 (VERIFIED):** SDT health effects small-to-moderate — not transformative.

## C. Limits of the Current Review

- **Window:** 2024-2026 primary (strict post-2024 = 2025+); pre-window literature covered only via Phase 1 anchors. Some 2026 items are very recent (Aug 2026) and unindexed elsewhere.
- **Source coverage:** OpenAlex/PubMed/arXiv/Semantic Scholar + web retrieval (Firecrawl). Paywalled full texts may be UNVERIFIED where abstract-only (BCTv1 methods, Pawson & Tilley 1997, Shove 2012, Ryan & Deci 2017, Miller & Rollnick 2013, Schatzki).
- **Quality-class heterogeneity (flagged per entry):** opinion articles (Jose 2025, Berzin & Topol 2025, Carey & Hogan 2025), preprints (Beacock 2026, Gutoreva 2026, SERUM, LongNAP, Agent Cybernetics, CALM-IT, ProEvent, UniClawBench, PROBE, ProAgent, CPE, ProACT), record-level-only verification (Teng 2025, Goh 2025, Wang & Wang 2024), single-country/single-site samples (Wong HK, Liu CN, Li CN), small-N (Beacock n=51, Liu N=32, Verhoeven n=20).
- **Discipline bias:** psychology/health literature dominates; HCI/STS/engineering strands present but under-sampled relative to their size; economics represented by one flagship (Brynjolfsson).
- **Retraction awareness:** one high-citation ChatGPT meta-analysis retracted (Wang & Fan 2025) — GenAI-education meta-analytic claims require cross-checking; one withdrawal (SRSUPM 2026).
- **Council method limits:** subagents fetched sources independently but did not cross-read each other's corpora; consolidation by the parent may have flattened nuances; no inter-rater reliability on coding choices.
- **Timing of review:** literature reviewed as of 2026-08-06; the field moves monthly.

## D. What This Means for the Architecture

1. **Hypothesis status, not doctrine.** Every diagnosis-to-selection step (COM-B component, TDF domain, BCT choice, SDT regulatory style, motivational state) is an inference with confidence — labeled, logged, user-correctable, outcome-arbitrated. The frameworks themselves are descriptive, not predictive (A1, A2).
2. **Skill preservation is a measured objective, not a slogan.** Assisted performance and independent capability diverge (B1); the agent must track unassisted-competence ("skill load") as a first-class metric and impose minimal sufficiency (hints, not answers) per Bastani's Tutor arm.
3. **Empowerment is engineered, not assumed.** Perceived agency ≠ capability (B2); friction can be protective; silence can be correct (ProACT); the agent's objective is user long-run capability.
4. **Autonomy preservation is a hard boundary.** SDT autonomy + MI spirit + empowerment research + von Foerster's imperative converge; MI without spirit fails (A4); performance-contingent rewards undermine intrinsic motivation (A3).
5. **Uncertainty is carried in the interface.** Low-confidence signals (17-26% prediction alignment, 52.6% MI coding) gate autonomous action: predictions inform timing, not execution; agent-facing claims inherit the evidence's calibration (modest effect sizes, heterogeneous samples).
6. **Version and witness conflicts are encoded as metadata.** TDF 12-vs-14, reward-undermining dispute, Beer-vs-Hui, enhancement-vs-erosion — preserved, not harmonized (Valens discipline).
7. **Philosophy stays philosophy.** Cybernetic closure/autopoiesis/technodiversity are design metaphors for LLM agents, not literal properties (A5); applicability claims are RECONSTRUCTED by default.
8. **Design for the durability gap.** AI-MI moves readiness, not behavior (B6); the architecture must schedule repeated, spaced, state-timed interventions and evaluate on sustained behavior, with explicit humility gates against single-session or single-prediction overclaim.
