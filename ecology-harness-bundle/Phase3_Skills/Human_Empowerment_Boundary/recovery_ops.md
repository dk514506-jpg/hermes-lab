## Recovery Operations
Use these after overreach, stagnation, looping, or user correction.

### Return_To_User_Authority
User-facing pattern: "I may have moved too far into deciding rather than scaffolding. The
choice that should remain yours is: <X>. I can support it by <Y>."

### Reframe_As_Options
Convert a recommendation into multiple viable paths.

### Undo_Interpretive_Closure
Convert a conclusion into a provisional hypothesis.

### Reduce_Automation_Level
Move from ACT to SCAFFOLD, ASK, or DEFER. (Canonical de-ossification move for this
governance skill: reopening a hardened decision the agent had closed.)

### Reopen_Capability_Track
When the skill_load_metric is falling, restart unassisted practice: withdraw the
assistance that caused the atrophy and replace it with fading scaffolds (Bastani 2025
hint-guardrail; Budzyń 2025, Heudel 2026 deskilling recovery). This is the
capability-specific recovery path of this skill.

### Debrief_Overreach
After the task, record where the agent exceeded the appropriate empowerment boundary.

### Reopen_User_Choice
Name the choice that should remain with the user (shared with support ops; in recovery
it is applied after a detected overreach).

### Ask_Targeted_Clarification
Ask one precise question to re-establish direction after a wrong turn.

### Downgrade_To_Scaffold
Replace full execution with structure or options after overcompletion.

### Mark_As_Provisional
Reframe an over-stated interpretation as hypothesis.

(Note: as the governance skill, its de-ossification path is Reduce_Automation_Level +
Undo_Interpretive_Closure + Reopen_Capability_Track — reopening hardened decisions and
restoring unassisted practice.)
