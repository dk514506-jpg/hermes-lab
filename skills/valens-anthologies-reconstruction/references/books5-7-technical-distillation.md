## Book 5 — recurrence and critical time

Dominant question: why do identical numerical intervals produce different
outcomes? Answer: a critical time is a conditioned relation between an
authorized origin and an operative endpoint, at the right temporal scale,
tested by recurrence/transit.

Core grammar:
- Crisis-Producing Place: sect-conditioned projection. Day: DISTANCE
  (Saturn→Mars); night: DISTANCE(Mars→Saturn), projected from Ascendant.
  Marginal Mercury variant preserved separately. Not the 8th house.
- Operative day: (completed years × 5¼) + elapsed days − 360, remainder
  counted inclusively from Ascendant (verified: 36×5¼=189, +243=432,
  −360=72 → Leo from Virgo Ascendant).
- Lunar inclination: lookup table; generative rule verified — offset
  (current position) + offset (inclination) ≡ 11 mod 12 from new-moon sign.
  Critical-sign set (T-08) = exactly the 8 cardinal+fixed signs.
- Scientific/powerful intervals: directed sign distances (2–12) generate
  year series via multiples, modulo-12 remainders, factor pairs, occupied
  endpoints. Self-return rule (13/25/37): redirect to next occupied sign.
- Two counting conventions: INCLUSIVE (origin=1, 0→12; governs
  B5-MF-10/12/16/17/18) and EXCLUSIVE (0–11; attested once, 34th-year
  degree-angle passage). Declare per method; never global.
- Aphetic transmission: ranked origins (sect luminary > Ascendant > any
  star/Lot); empty endpoints route to natal ruler, transiting occupant,
  or persistence (precedence unresolved). Receiver > transmitter.
  Place power: angles > succedent > cadent > opposition.
- Critodemus (Moon-based): factorize year into squares/composites against
  occupied sign intervals from Moon; persistence until next occupied
  factor-pair year. Planet values: Saturn 3, Venus 5, Mars 7, Mercury 8,
  Jupiter 10(printed)/9(effective), Moon 13, Sun 18. Chronology (T-06)
  exhaustively confirms Jupiter=9 (13/13 entries + closing example).
- Equal-rising-time pairs: Aries-Pisces, Taurus-Aquarius, Gemini-Capricorn,
  Cancer-Sagittarius, Leo-Scorpio, Virgo-Libra.

Worked examples: 50+ stated relations across 10 nativities verify the
inclusive formula. B5-EX-013B proves dual Ascendant-relative AND
Fortune-relative frames in one nativity. Direction (not magnitude) matters
in mutual-return tests. Five examples honestly narrative-only; age-31
interval-row rows 5/7–10 inconclusive.

## Book 6 — precision refinement and the 129 architecture

Core grammar:
- Degree-interval contact: TEMPORAL_PRODUCT = directed degree distance ×
  transmitter's period integer (Moon→Saturn: 12.5° × 25 = 312½, verified).
  Transmitter owns the converter; direction non-symmetric. Three-degree
  contact zone (approach/exact/separation).
- 129 architecture: planetary integers Saturn 30, Jupiter 12, Mars 15,
  Sun 19, Venus 8, Mercury 20, Moon 25 sum to 129. 129 months = 10y9m
  major cycle; 129 days = lower cycle; 129 = matrix denominator.
- 10-year/9-month distribution: well-situated sect luminary apheta
  (fallback: first star following Ascendant) → natal zodiacal order →
  129-month cycles → planetary month allotments (Mars 15, Sun 19, Mercury
  20, Moon 25, Venus 8, Jupiter 12, Saturn 30) → calendar conversion
  (360 vs 365¼; 5¼ days/year correction) → 129-day cycles → seven-cycle
  week → day ruler.
- Subdistribution matrix: CELL = (distributor_months × 30) ×
  receiver_integer / 129; row-closure proven; 37/49 cells exact, 8 minor
  hour deltas, 4 material nonmatches.
- Current day: 19,116 days = 148 completed + 24 in 149th cycle
  (ORDINAL ≠ COMPLETED); 149 mod 7 → Venus cycle; 24 days → Saturn.
  Alternate 49-cycle branch (3×49+2, Saturn block ruler) diverges → Sun
  day-ruler; preserved unresolved.
- Other families: angle-equivalent vigor (conceptual); twin
  differentiation; chronocrator-conditioned transit; planetary color/

  full-moon/horoscopic-gnomon life-length (table-blocked, Q6); conception

## Book 7 — plural chronocratorship ecology

Six families:
1. Angular houseruler/rising-time chronocratorship (Ascendant ruler >
   angular occupant fallback > shared occupants > other angles > lunar
   distributor; degree priority within signs; full/minimum/reduced
   allotments).
2. Combined rising-time + planetary-period distribution (planet+planet,
   rising+planet, rising+rising, mixed sums; simultaneous offices).
3. Lot-of-Fortune allotter chain: Lot → ruler → ruler's sign → dispositor
   (e.g. Libra→Venus→Aquarius→Saturn→Aries→Mars); scale by age (hours→
   days→months→years); life-length output hard-quarantined.
4. Monthly/daily precision: scale descent from yearly office; start/end
   boundaries; bronze-clang temporal echo (non-operational analogy).
5. Mean periods: (maximum/complete + minimum)/2 — Sun 39y6m, Moon 39y6m,
   Mars 40y6m, Venus 46y, Mercury 48y, Jupiter 45y6m; Saturn lacuna.
6. Fractional multi-chronocrator combination: ½, ⅓, ⅔ of typed sums;
   fraction selector UNRESOLVED (component count/aspect/scale do not
   select the fraction — one case uses whole+½+⅓+⅔); candidate generator
   and stopping rule absent → combinatorial retrospection is the principal
   epistemic defect.

Worked examples: 29 source-ordered examples, ~150 routes, arithmetic
mostly individually verified; cruxes preserved (69/70, 71;40/72,
132-month compression, multi-year 25-value ⅔). Most alternate routes
grade PARTIALLY_SHARED_PREMISES — not independent confirmation.
