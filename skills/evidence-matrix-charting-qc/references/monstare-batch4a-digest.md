## Cross-row recall examples (batch 4a)

- A1-07 Stiegler → A1-08 Feenberg (pharmacological frame for democratic rationalization of technology)
- A1-05 Simondon → A1-11 Winner tension: Simondon's technical-object genesis (non-economic, internal normativity) vs. Winner's artifact-politics (artifacts inscribe political priorities, including economic-political)
- A1-15 Heidegger → HUI-2024: Hui's cosmotechnics as direct response to Heidegger's enframing/Gestell — the cosmotechnics project begins where Heidegger identifies the problem
- A1-02 Hui → A1-01/HUI-2024: definitional continuity check — "cosmotechnics = unification of cosmos + moral through technical activity" confirmed consistent across A1-01, A1-02, HUI-2024
- A1-18 Haraway cyborg → A1-05 Simondon concretization: both refuse the purity boundary (organism/machine for Haraway; abstract/concrete for Simondon), but Haraway celebrates hybridity as political while Simondon asks about the *genesis* of the concrete
- A1-12 Latour → A1-11 Winner: both artifacts-as-actors, but Latour's missing-masses is a sociology-of-science move (non-humans in the social bond) while Winner's is a politics-of-artifacts move (artifacts have politics)

## Vocabulary to carry forward

- Simondon: concretization, margin of indeterminacy, element/individual/ensemble, abstract vs concrete technical object, technicity as phase
- Stiegler: pharmakon, epiphylogenesis, grammatization, originary technicity, exteriorization, Prometheus/Epimetheus, Dasein's technical constitution
- Heidegger: Gestell (enframing), Bestand (standing-reserve), poiesis (bringing-forth), aletheia (unconcealment), destining (Geschick)
- Hui: cosmotechnics as cosmos+moral+technics unification, multiple cosmotechnics, Anthropocene as cosmopolitical
- Winner: artifacts have politics, inscription, inherently political technologies, two modes (settle issue / require political form)
- Haraway: cyborg ontology, three boundary breakdowns (human/animal, organism-machine, physical/non-physical), informatics of domination
- Latour: missing masses, non-humans as social actors, delegation/prescription, frozen morality
