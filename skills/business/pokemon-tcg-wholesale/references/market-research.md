# Market Research Notes (gathered 2026-08)

Condensed from reseller guides and community sources. Benchmarks, not gospel — verify against live TCGPlayer/eBay data before acting.

## Bulk rate benchmarks (closo.co 2026 guide; Quora wholesale answers)

- Commons/Uncommons: **$15–20 per 1,000 cards**
- Code cards (online pack codes): ~$0.35–0.50 each
- Holo rares: $0.05–0.08 each
- V / ex / Ultra Rares: $0.50–0.75 each
- "Most worthless Rare" in bulk: ~$1 each
- Sealed case of booster boxes (216 packs): ~$600 → ~$2.75/pack vs $4.25 retail per pack

## Bulk buyers (ship flat-rate, they PayPal you)

Safari Zone, Full Grip Games, Derium's. Fill a form, ship flat-rate box, get paid. Do NOT list bulk as individual lots — documented failure: sorting "lots of 50" for eBay netted ~$1.50/hour.

## Wholesale/distributor requirements (Quora, George North + assistant answer)

True wholesale requires ALL of:
1. Registered business (LLC or sole proprietor) — wholesalers don't sell to individuals
2. State registration + Tax ID / resale certificate
3. Case-quantity minimums (not by pack/box)

US distributors: Alliance Game Distributors, Southern Hobby Supply, GTS Distribution. Directory: cardboardconnection.com/trading-card-distributors.
Other sources: liquidation pallets (Liquidation.com, B-Stock, Direct Liquidation — steep discounts, uncertain condition), estate buys, FB Marketplace arbitrage. Counterfeit risk high on Alibaba/AliExpress "retail pack" suppliers.

## Platform economics

| Channel | Use for | Fees / take |
|---|---|---|
| eBay | High-end + mid singles (>$20); sub-$20 viable via Standard Envelope ($0.64–1.12) | ~13% |
| TCGPlayer | Playable/meta volume (players buy 4x for decks) | ~12–15% |
| Local Game Store | Speed only — pays 40–60% of market | "convenience tax", avoid |
| Bulk buyers | Commons/energy by the thousand | low, volume |

Pricing references: TCGPlayer Market Price (raw singles standard), eBay **sold/completed** filter (never active listings — ask prices lie), PriceCharting (graded history), PSA population.

## Grading math

- Cost: $15–25/card, turnaround months.
- Rule: grade ONLY if (Value of Graded 10 − Grading Fee) > Value of Raw card.
- Documented loss: Charizard VMAX raw $100, PSA 9 $105, fee $25 → net −$20.

## Meta-game arbitrage (the big controllable lever)

Tournament results spike card prices. Documented case: Iron Hands ex stack at $8 → $18 as a regional tournament approached (players scrambling to build decks). Watch: competitive deck lists, TCGPlayer market price, search-volume signals.

## Condition & shipping protocol (scam defense)

- Photograph front + back; dark background shows edge whitening.
- eBay "Scan to List" defaults to Mint — always verify actual condition or eat return claims.
- Packaging: penny sleeve → top loader / card saver → team bag → cardboard sandwich → tracking (>$20 mandatory).
- High-value: video of packing + package weight on receipt wins "Item Not Received" disputes (documented $400 claim won this way).

## Collections buying rule

Binder with 500 cards, 10 worth $20+: pull the 10, sell individually; sell the remaining 490 as "partial binder lot". Selling the whole binder = buyer pays only for the 10, treats the rest as free bulk.
