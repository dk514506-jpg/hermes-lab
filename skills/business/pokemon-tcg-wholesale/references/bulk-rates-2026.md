## The rules that matter more than the rates

1. **The $1 Rule:** pull every card that scans/looks up ≥ $1 and sell it separately. "A stack of 50 cards worth $1–5 each is worth $50–250 total, which might be more than the remaining 4,950 commons combined." (Misprint)
2. **Sorted bulk sells for 2–3× unsorted** — category/era separation is the highest-value sorting step; complete common/uncommon sets carry an additional premium to set builders.
3. **Unsorted bulk gets the lowest rates.** Sorting is the leverage; the buylist is the floor you beat by sorting.
4. **Condition gate:** buylists reject non-NM. Condition category step-down ≈ 5–10% per grade (Heck et al. 2026, PLOS ONE; also Cavalletti/Weber for MTG).
5. **Benchmark the middleman channel against the buylist:** if the Seattle/SF chain's effective rate on sorted bulk doesn't beat buylist + shipping, the channel has a structural leak.
