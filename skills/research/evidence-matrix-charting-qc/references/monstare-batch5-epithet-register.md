# Epithet Register Structure and Examples

Reference for the Epithet Register (`/opt/data/Monstare_epithet_register.md`).

## Purpose

The register preserves each charted author's voice — not just their claims, but their tonal signature, their most concentrated phrases, and the felt sense of what they are pointing at. Downstream artifacts (Design Veto Catalogue, Cosmotechnic Audit Card, pilot rules) draw from this register so that a rule grounded in Heidegger *sounds* Heideggerian, not flattened into generic management-speak.

## Per-author structure

- **Epithet** — 1-3 word signature that calls up the whole body
- **Quotations** — range from subtle (quiet, precise, understated) to robust (prophetic, sweeping, declarative); the tonal register is exemplified, never described
- **Reverie** — evocative vignette (100-200 words) conveying the full spectrum of feelings and thoughts on the concept

## Worked examples

### Heidegger, *The Question Concerning Technology* (1949/1977)

**Epithet:** Enframing (Gestell)

**Quotations:**
> "The essence of technology is nothing technological."
> "Technology is therefore no mere means. Technology is a way of revealing."
> "Where everything that presences exhibits itself in the light of a cause-effect coherence, even God can lose all that is exalted."

**Reverie:** A hydroelectric plant is set into the current of the Rhine. The river is dammed up into the power works. What the river is now — a water power supplier — derives from out of the essence of the power station. The Rhine as uttered out of Hölderlin's hymn is gone. Enframing conceals not only bringing-forth; it conceals revealing itself. Where danger is, grows the saving power also.

### Borgmann, *Technology and the Character of Contemporary Life* (1984)

**Epithet:** Device Paradigm

**Quotations:**
> "The device paradigm is the defining structure of modern technological culture."
> "A device is a means that makes the end available without engagement."
> "We are surrounded by commodified outputs stripped of the practices that would make them meaningful."

**Reverie:** The fireplace demands wood, demands tending, demands presence. The central heat makes warmth available without engagement. The commodity arrives. The practice is gone. We are left with outputs without orbit, comfort without center.

### Haraway, *A Cyborg Manifesto* (1985/1991)

**Epithet:** Cyborg

**Quotations:**
> "A cyborg is a cybernetic organism, a hybrid of machine and organism."
> "By the late twentieth century, our time, a mythic time, we are all chimeras."
> "Though both are bound in the spiral dance, I would rather be a cyborg than a goddess."

**Reverie:** The boundary between human and animal is thoroughly breached. We are chimeras, theorized and fabricated hybrids of machine and organism. The cyborg has no origin story in the Western sense — no Garden of Eden, no phallic mother. It skips the step of original unity.
