## Platform / route economics
- eBay ~13% fees; TCGPlayer ~12–15%; PWE (plain white envelope) shipping $0.64–1.12 under $20 vs ~$4 Ground.
- LGS pays 40–60% of market = the "convenience tax" to avoid.
- Seattle middleman channel 4–8% = liquidity floor; direct routes capture 2–5× bulk on most tiers.
- Grading: only when (graded-10 value − $15–25 fee) > raw value. Almost never for bulk ops.
- Shipment disputes: packing video + weight receipt ≥$20; tracking ≥$20.

## Sourcing & negotiation levers
- End-of-show discounts (dealers hate packing out) — strongest, least used; perfect for bulky sheets.
- Bundle negotiation: 5–10% off from 10–20% of dealers at any show; cash talks.
- Collections at 70–80% of market value = standard sourcing model for resellers.
- Condition rule of thumb: one grade step down ≈ −5–10% (Heck et al. 2026 / Cavalletti via Weber).
- True wholesale (distributors) requires a registered business + resale cert + case quantities.

## Meta timing
Tournament-relevant playables spike ahead of regionals (documented: Iron Hands ex $8 → $18
pre-regional, CLOSO 2026). Hold meta tier with a thesis; auto-alert on deltas.
