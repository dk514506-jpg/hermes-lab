## 3. Fault lines (must not be smoothed over)

1. **DES vs MPI tension (A5-06):** DES captures momentary, pristine, visual detail; MPI captures temporal extension and engagement. If the Monstare protocol needs BOTH momentary sampling AND extended sense-making, it must integrate both methods — not choose one.

2. **A3-02 umbrella-construct critique vs A3-01/A2-01 usage:** Bennett et al. show that "agency" and "autonomy" are used vaguely across HCI. But A3-01 (Limerick et al.) and A2-01 (Li et al.) use these terms without explicit definitions. The tension: the critique says "be specific," but the field (including some charted rows) is not.

3. **A8-02 ritual mechanism vs A8-03/A8-04 ritual structure:** Norton & Gino identify control restoration as the mechanism; Van Gennep and Turner identify separation-liminality-incorporation as the structure. Mechanism and structure are complementary but distinct — the Ritual Transition Cards need both.

4. **A2-02 Lupton critique vs A2-01 Li et al. model:** Lupton critiques self-tracking as datafying selfhood and embedding neoliberal optimization. Li et al. propose a design model (collection → reflection → action) without addressing the critique. The tension: can you design a "reflective" tool that doesn't also optimize?

## 4. Tier-C pilot implications (binding)

- **Phenomenological capture (A5-01 Varela):** The tool must capture first-person experience alongside metrics — not as an afterthought, but as a structural requirement. The Phenomenological Registration Sheet is mandatory.

- **Second-person interview (A5-02 Petitmengin):** The registration interface must use open, specific, non-leading prompts that help the user re-live a particular episode. Episode-evoking, not opinion-evoking.

- **DES momentary sampling (A5-05 Hurlburt & Heavey):** The tool must include a random or semi-random beep that prompts the user to capture their experience at the moment of the beep — pristine, not retrospective.

- **DES vs MPI integration (A5-06 Bass-Krueger):** The tool must integrate both momentary sampling (DES) and extended sense-making (MPI). For flow-state detection, DES; for retrospective sense-making, MPI.

- **Ritual control restoration (A8-02 Norton & Gino):** The tool must include ritual structures that restore the user's sense of control during transitions, interruptions, and losses. The ritual need not be religious or belief-dependent.

- **Explicit agency definitions (A3-02 Bennett et al.):** The Agency & Autonomy Checklist must require explicit definitions of what "agency" and "autonomy" mean for each tool/artifact.
