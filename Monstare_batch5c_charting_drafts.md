## A8-07 — Tian et al., Enacting Rituals to Improve Self-Control (2017)
**Source:** HBS PDF (full article). Text layer verified. Complete.
**Pri B | CR medium | Function: EMPIRICAL/MECHANISM | Evidence Type: empirical | Causal Status: causal**

**Key Finding / Thesis:**
Enacting ritualized actions can enhance subjective feelings of self-discipline, such that rituals can be harnessed to improve behavioral self-control. Six experiments show: a pre-eating ritual over 5 days reduced calorie intake; pairing a ritual with healthy eating increased healthy food choices; the effect held even when gestures were not labeled as ritual; the mechanism is heightened feelings of self-discipline. Rituals affect self-control specifically because they alter responses to self-control conflicts.

**Effect Size / Strength:** causal (lab-bounded, experimental). Strength = high as the EMPIRICAL/MECHANISM row — the self-discipline mechanism is the explicit warrant for the Monstare Protocol vNext's ritual structures (construct-role claim).

**Limitations:** Lab-bounded experiments — the rituals are simple, short, and imposed. Long-term adherence and ecological validity are uncertain. The sample is US university students.

**Disconfirming Implication:** If rituals do NOT increase self-discipline (if the mechanism is something else — e.g., placebo, demand), the self-control thesis fails.

**H1:** yes | **H2:** partial
**Design Implication:** Ritual as self-regulatory structure. The tool must include ritualized sequences that enhance the user's self-discipline during self-control conflicts (e.g., task-switching, interruption recovery).
**Cosmotechnic Implication:** Structure aids restraint. The cosmotechnic audit must include the ritual dimension — the structured, symbolic practices that aid self-regulation.
**Causal Status:** causal (lab-bounded, experimental)

**H1:** no | **H2:** partial

**Cosmotechnic Implication:** Ritual binds groups. The cosmotechnic audit must include the social dimension — the practices that unite or divide communities.
