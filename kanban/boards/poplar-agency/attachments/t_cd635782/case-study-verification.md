# Chicago merchant case-study verification

Research date: 2026-08-11 UTC

Status vocabulary:
- **Verified**: directly stated on an official merchant site, a named local-news source, or a platform listing whose page was retrieved.
- **Unverified**: plausible lead found in a search result or directory, but not sufficiently corroborated or not retrievable from the underlying page.
- **Inference**: interpretation derived from verified facts, not a merchant-stated fact.
- **Merchant question**: should be confirmed directly with the owner/operator before use in a proposal, case study, or campaign.

## 2. Chicago Sugar Daddy Patisserie

### Verified facts

- **Verified — location and contact.** The official ordering site identifies “Chicago Sugar Daddy Patisserie LLC” at 3243 N Broadway St, Chicago, IL 60657-3561, phone (773) 939-3542.[1]
- **Verified — published operating schedule (as retrieved).** The official site lists Monday and Tuesday closed; Wednesday and Thursday 12:00–8:00 pm; Friday and Saturday 10:00 am–8:00 pm; Sunday 10:00 am–5:00 pm.[1]
- **Verified — product/brand activity.** The official site links the business’s Instagram account and displays recent examples of custom cakes, including Pokémon/Charizard, music-artist/event cakes, and Pride-themed cakes.[1]
- **Verified — ownership and concept, with source attribution.** WGN-TV identifies partners James Cox and Ryan Ibasco as the people behind Chicago Sugar Daddy and describes Filipino recipes meeting American classics, with a focus on love and community.[2]
- **Verified — neighborhood positioning in 2023.** WGN-TV says the patisserie is in Chicago’s Lakeview neighborhood and describes it as being at the corner of Broadway and Melrose; the article was posted June 23, 2023.[2]
- **Verified — public social-search lead for founding year.** A search result for the business’s Instagram owner video states that Rayan and James are the owners and “started in 2021.” This is a merchant/social claim surfaced in search, but the Instagram page itself was not independently extractable in this pass.[8]

### Unverified / do not overstate

- **Unverified — exact legal ownership spelling.** WGN uses “Ryan Ibasco”; the Instagram search result uses “Rayan” and the official legal text says “Chicago Sugar Daddy Patisserie LLC.” Confirm the owners’ preferred names and legal entity.
- **Unverified — 2021 opening date.** Treat “started in 2021” as a lead, not a fully corroborated opening date. It may mean the brand began, not that the storefront opened.
- **Unverified — revenue, customer count, repeat rate, campaign conversion, or profitability.** No public source retrieved provides these operating metrics.

### Inference

- The combination of custom celebration cakes, culturally specific flavor identity, public event/Pride expression, and a neighborhood storefront suggests a strong “occasion + identity + community” marketing position. This is a strategic interpretation, not a merchant quote.
- Custom cakes and after-party/event cakes likely create higher-consideration, preordered demand than ordinary walk-in pastry demand. Validate average order value, lead time, and margin before designing an offer.

### Merchant questions

1. Did the business begin in 2021, and when did the Broadway storefront open?
2. What share of sales is custom cakes vs. walk-in pastry/loaves/slices?
3. Which occasions or communities produce the highest-margin repeat orders?
4. Are event partnerships (clubs, Pride, DJs, venues, corporate clients) repeatable acquisition channels?
5. What follow-up permission is collected after an order, and what percentage reorders within 30/90 days?

## 3. Momo Factory

### Verified facts

- **Verified — business category/positioning.** The official site presents Momo Factory as a Nepalese/Himalayan restaurant and says customers can order “home-style cooking.”[3]
- **Verified — two current locations listed by the official site.** The site lists 3202 N Broadway, Chicago, IL 60657 (Lakeview) and 860 N Orleans St, Chicago, IL 60610 (Near North/River North).[3]
- **Verified — location phone numbers and hours on official site.** Broadway: (773) 661-2970, Tuesday–Sunday 11:00 am–9:30 pm, Monday closed. Orleans: (872) 342-2031, Tuesday–Sunday 11:00 am–9:00 pm, Monday closed.[3]
- **Verified — official menu/order and catering pathways.** The site links separate ordering pages for both locations, a catering menu PDF, catering, and events.[3]
- **Verified — chef/staff claim.** The About Us page states that the chef has 20 years of experience cooking in fine restaurants; it also says the restaurant handles private events and catering and sources fresh ingredients from local farmers’ markets.[4]
- **Verified — second-location announcement.** The official homepage explicitly says the new location at 860 N Orleans St is open.[3]
- **Verified — LinkedIn public profile lead for founding year.** Search results identify the company as privately held, headquartered in Chicago, and founded in 2019, with a primary location at 3202 N Broadway. The LinkedIn page was not extractable, so treat the founding year as a corroboration lead rather than a fully inspected source.[5]

### Unverified / do not overstate

- **Unverified — founding year 2019.** Public search metadata says 2019, but the underlying LinkedIn page could not be retrieved. Confirm with the operator or an archived official announcement.
- **Unverified — opening date of the Orleans location.** The official site says it is open but does not state the opening date in the retrieved text.
- **Unverified — “famous” / “authentic” as objective market claims.** The official site uses language such as “Chicago’s Famous Nepalese Cuisine”; use this as brand positioning, not an independently verified ranking or authenticity judgment.
- **Unverified — performance metrics, delivery mix, catering share, and customer retention.** No retrieved source provides them.

### Inference

- Momo Factory has a natural neighborhood-network opportunity because it combines two neighborhood locations, catering, events, and a distinctive cuisine category. A cross-location passport or event/catering referral loop could be tested, but the economics and operational capacity need merchant confirmation.
- The second location may indicate expansion and a need to route customers between neighborhoods, but expansion motive and success cannot be inferred as fact from the site alone.

### Merchant questions

1. What was the exact opening date of the original location and of 860 N Orleans?
2. Is the 2019 founding year correct, and what was the original format (pop-up, delivery, storefront)?
3. What are the best-margin items and busiest slow periods at each location?
4. How much demand comes from dine-in, pickup, delivery, catering, and private events?
5. Can a shared neighborhood passport reward visits across both locations without cannibalizing normal orders?

## 4. Lakeview Elegant Salon

### Verified facts

- **Verified — location and booking channel.** The salon’s booking site/Fresha page identifies Lakeview Elegant Salon at 3428 N Broadway, Chicago, Illinois.[6]
- **Verified — service categories and listed prices (retrieved page).** Fresha lists Men Haircut ($35/30 min), haircut/beard trim/hot towel shave/shampoo ($60/1 hour), Women’s Haircut and wash (from $60/1 hour), and beard trim/hot towel shave ($25/30 min).[6]
- **Verified — team names/roles shown publicly.** Fresha lists Jack (Barber), Jonathan (Stylist), Salwa (Stylist), and George (Barber).[6]
- **Verified — booking-site positioning.** Fresha describes the salon as offering personalized beauty services and identifies it as near East Lakeview; the page also lists pet-friendly, kid-friendly, wheelchair-accessible, parking, and public-transport attributes.[6]
- **Verified — operating hours on Fresha as retrieved.** Monday closed; Tuesday–Saturday 10:00 am–7:00 pm; Sunday 10:00 am–5:00 pm.[6]
- **Verified — independent directory footprint.** Yelp identifies the business at 3428 N Broadway, Chicago, IL 60657, in Hair Salons and Barbers, with four reviews and a 5.0 rating on the retrieved page.[7]
- **Verified — public Yelp status.** Yelp labels the listing “Unclaimed,” so the listing should not be treated as an owner-controlled source.[7]

### Unverified / conflicting

- **Unverified — official website ownership/control.** Yelp displays lakeviewelegantsalon.com as a website, but the retrieved booking page is Fresha and does not establish who controls the domain.
- **Unverified — phone number.** Search metadata and directory results show (312) 714-4188, but the retrieved Fresha page did not display it. Confirm directly before publication.
- **Unverified — owner identity and opening date.** Neither retrieved primary/secondary page identifies the owner or opening date.
- **Conflicting directory data — Sunday hours.** Fresha lists Sunday 10:00 am–5:00 pm; Yelp’s retrieved page lists Sunday closed. Confirm current hours with the salon and prefer the booking system for appointment planning only after checking with the merchant.
- **Unverified — review volume/quality beyond the retrieved pages.** A separate search snippet claimed a much larger review count, but it was not used because the underlying source was not verified and conflicts with Yelp’s four reviews.

### Inference

- The salon’s combined barber/stylist team and service menu could support a “household/party-prep” or cross-service loyalty program, but this is a hypothesis—not evidence that customers currently cross-buy.
- The publicly listed team of four suggests a possible capacity constraint or opportunity around filling specific practitioner schedules. Verify chair utilization and no-show rates before proposing slow-period promotions.

### Merchant questions

1. Who owns/operates the salon, and when did it open at 3428 N Broadway?
2. Which hours and phone number are current? Why does Sunday information differ across directories?
3. Which services have the most availability and the highest contribution margin?
4. Do clients book recurring visits, and can rebooking happen before checkout?
5. Is the salon interested in cross-referrals with nearby restaurants, events, or other Lakeview businesses?

## Cross-case synthesis (inference only)

- The strongest verified public material is currently for Chicago Sugar Daddy and Momo Factory because both have official sites plus local/directory corroboration.
- Lakeview Elegant Salon has a useful service/team/booking record, but weaker ownership, opening-date, phone, and hours certainty.
- Dots and Dashes is not safely identifiable from public search alone; it should remain an intake lead, not a factual case study.
- Across all four, public sources do not establish incremental visits, repeat purchase rates, customer lifetime value, margin, or campaign ROI. Any deck claiming those outcomes would require merchant data.
