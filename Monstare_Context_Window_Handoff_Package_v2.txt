NON-NEGOTIABLE PROJECT DISCIPLINES
1. Citation is not evidence.
A matrix row is not charted until readable-source identity has been verified and the row has been read enough to populate key finding/thesis, strength/effect size where applicable, limitations, and disconfirming implication.

2. Tier-P must stay visible.
Do not treat Monstare as empirically established. It is plausible and architecturally grounded, not proven.

3. Protected-floor stakes govern decisions.
S1 Telic, S7 Moral, and S10 Reversibility cannot be traded away for gains on other stakes.

4. Do not collapse H2 into productivity.
H2 is a vector of quality, judgment, ecology, technology, phenomenology, and reversibility, not a scalar output measure.

5. Phenomenology-before-calibration remains law.
Operation is experiential. Capture is phenomenological. Calibration is refinement. Do not interpret while capturing or reduce lived experience to data before preservation.

6. Locus must check interfaces.
Every artifact should identify its modular interfaces: source -> claim, claim -> matrix row, matrix row -> design implication, design implication -> stake consequence, stake consequence -> artifact or pilot rule.

7. Burden is itself a stake.
If the research apparatus becomes too heavy to use, S9 fires. Do not solve rigor by adding uncontrolled mass.
